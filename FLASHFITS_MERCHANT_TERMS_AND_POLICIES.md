## 1. Introduction & Scope of Agreement
This Merchant Partner Agreement ("Agreement") constitutes a binding legal contract between **FlashFits Quick Commerce Private Limited** ("FlashFits", "Platform", "we", "us") and the merchant entity or individual boutique proprietor ("Merchant", "Store Partner", "you"). 

By registering an account on the FlashFits Merchant App / Merchant Module, uploading inventory, listing catalogs, or fulfilling customer orders, you unconditionally agree to adhere to all terms, operating guidelines, quality benchmarks, and Service Level Agreements (SLAs) set forth herein.

---

## 2. Merchant Eligibility, Onboarding & Physical Store Verification
To maintain platform integrity and consumer trust:
- **Commercial KYC:** Merchants must submit a verified Permanent Account Number (PAN), Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) where legally required, and an active bank account matching the registered trade entity.
- **Physical Desk Verification:** Stores must provide exact geo-coordinates (latitude/longitude) of their physical storefront or fulfillment desk for precision rider dispatch.
- **Staffing & Timings:** The merchant's pickup desk must be staffed during operating hours declared in the Merchant Module.

---

## 3. Product Catalog, Authenticity & Quality Standards
- **100% Genuine Apparel & Merchandise:** Counterfeits, unauthorized replicas, first copies, or grey-market goods are strictly prohibited. Listing counterfeit merchandise incurs an immediate ₹50,000 contractual penalty per incident, permanent store deactivation, and referral to law enforcement authorities.
- **New & Factory-Fresh Condition:** All listed garments, footwear, and lifestyle goods must be brand new, unwashed, unworn, and bearing original manufacturer price and barcode tags.
- **Price Parity:** The listed retail price on FlashFits shall never exceed the in-store offline retail price or Maximum Retail Price (MRP).

---

## 4. Order Fulfillment SLAs (Try & Buy Local Orders)
To ensure the customer promise of 30-minute doorstep fashion delivery:
1. **5-Minute Order Acceptance SLA:** Merchants must acknowledge incoming order notifications within 5 minutes.
2. **10-Minute Order Packing SLA:** Once accepted, items must be retrieved from inventory, inspected, folded, and sealed inside official FlashFits Zip Covers within 10 minutes.
3. **Rider Handshake Protocol:** Merchants must verify the rider's assigned Order ID last 4 digits (`#XXXX`) before exchanging the 4-digit pickup OTP.

---

## 5. The Try & Buy Model & Doorstep Trial Protocol
- **₹0 Upfront Payment:** Customers select multiple styles and sizes to try at home with ₹0 upfront payment.
- **Dedicated Doorstep Trial:** A FlashFits delivery rider waits outside the customer's doorstep while the customer fits the apparel.
- **Partial Keeps & Instant Returns:** The customer marks which items to keep and which to return in their app. Payment is collected only for kept garments + delivery fee.
- **Zero Merchant Loss on Partial Keeps:** Merchants earn full margin on all retained garments. Unkept items are brought back to the store immediately by the same rider.

---

## 6. Return Verification, Damage Inspection & Merchant Protection Fund (MPF)
1. **Immediate In-Person Handback:** When the rider returns to your store with unkept trial merchandise, you must immediately inspect items in the rider's presence.
2. **Return Acceptance Criteria:**
   - Original brand/price tags intact.
   - Clean, odorless, free of makeup or perfume stains, snags, or tears.
   - 4-digit return completion OTP shared with the rider only after physical verification.
3. **24-Hour Damage Claim Window:** If an item is returned damaged or missing tags, submit a Damage Incident Report via the Merchant Module within 24 hours with high-resolution photo/video evidence.
4. **Merchant Protection Fund Reimbursement:** Verified transit or trial damage is reimbursed at the merchant's wholesale landing cost within 3 business days.

---

## 7. Courier Delivery Orders (Standard Shipping)
- **48-Hour Dispatch SLA:** Orders destined for distant/courier shipping must be packaged and handed to the designated logistics carrier within 48 hours.
- **72-Hour (3-Day) Customer Return Window:** Courier customers may request a return within 3 calendar days of delivery for defective or sizing issues.
- **Merchant Inspection SLA:** The merchant has 48 hours from receipt of the reverse courier parcel to inspect the item before refund release.

---

## 8. Commission Structure, Settlement Cycles & Taxation
- **Commission on Kept Items Only:** Platform commission is calculated strictly on Net Kept Sales. ₹0 commission is charged on items returned during trial.
- **T+2 Rolling Settlement:** Completed payouts are remitted directly to the merchant's bank account on a T+2 business day cycle.
- **Tax Deductions (TCS & TDS):** 1% TCS under Section 52 of the CGST/SGST Act and 1% TDS under Section 194-O of the Income Tax Act are deducted and deposited to government tax portals. Monthly GST invoices are issued by the 10th of every month.

---

## 9. Packaging, FlashFits Zip Covers & Security Barcode Seals
- **Mandatory Branded Covers:** All Try & Buy orders must be dispatched in official FlashFits Zip Covers. Polybags or plain bags are prohibited.
- **Serialized Security Seal Tags:** Each zipper must be locked with a serialized tamper-evident barcode seal. Broken or missing seals upon rider/customer arrival constitute grounds for immediate order rejection.
- **Supply Replenishment:** Zip covers and security seal tags can be ordered at subsidized rates directly from the Merchant Module.

---

## 10. Customer Data Confidentiality & Non-Solicitation Policy
- **DPDP Act Compliance:** Customer contact details are shared strictly for real-time delivery coordination.
- **Strict Non-Solicitation:** Direct marketing via WhatsApp, SMS, phone calls, or placing offline store promotional flyers inside delivery packages is strictly prohibited.
- **Penalties:** Misuse of customer data leads to immediate store deactivation and damages under the Indian Information Technology Act.

---

## 11. Store Health, Penalties & Disciplinary Action
- **Merchant Cancellation Penalty:** Cancelling an order after acceptance incurs a penalty of ₹100 or 10% of order value.
- **Auto-Offline:** Stores missing 3 consecutive order broadcasts are automatically set to Offline.

---

## 12. Suspension, Termination & Dispute Resolution
- **Voluntary Termination:** 15-day written notice.
- **Immediate Suspension:** Incurred for counterfeit merchandise, abusive conduct, or fraud.
- **Arbitration & Governing Law:** Subject to the Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of Bangalore/New Delhi, India.
