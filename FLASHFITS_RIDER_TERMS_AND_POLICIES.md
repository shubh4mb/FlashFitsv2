## 1. Engagement & Independent Contractor Relationship
- **Independent Partner Status:** You are engaged as an independent delivery service contractor. Nothing in this agreement shall be construed to create an employment, joint venture, or agency relationship between you and FlashFits.
- **Freedom of Flexibility:** Delivery partners retain full discretion over when to log online, which operating slots to choose, and when to conclude shifts.

---

## 2. Partner Eligibility & Required Documentation
To deliver on the FlashFits platform, riders must maintain active and verifiable:
- Valid Indian Driving License for two-wheelers (geared/gearless).
- Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC) & active motor third-party insurance.
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) & Aadhaar identity verification.
- Active bank account registered in the partner's legal name for receiving payouts.
- Smartphone running Android 10+ / iOS 15+ with operational GPS and camera for delivery verification.

---

## 3. Order Assignment, Dispatch & Store Pickup SLA
1. **15–30 Second Acceptance Window:** Partners have up to 30 seconds to accept incoming broadcast alerts.
2. **10–15 Minute Store Arrival:** Travel promptly to the partner boutique/warehouse using in-app turn-by-turn navigation.
3. **Store Pickup Handshake:** Share the last 4 digits of the Order ID (`#XXXX`) with the store manager and collect the 4-digit pickup OTP.
4. **Pre-Departure Inspection:** Verify that the FlashFits Zip Cover is sealed with an untampered security barcode tag. Never accept an unsealed or damaged package.

---

## 4. Try & Buy Doorstep Trial Protocol (Rider Obligations)
1. **Doorstep Arrival & OTP Handshake:** Greet the customer professionally, verify the 4-digit delivery handshake OTP in the Rider App, and hand over the sealed Zip Cover.
2. **Designated Doorstep Waiting Window:** Wait patiently outside the customer's residence for up to 15–30 minutes while the customer fits the apparel.
   - *Never enter the customer's private residence or fitting room.*
   - *Doorstep waiting time is compensated through dedicated wait-time incentives.*
3. **Post-Trial Selection & Reconciliation:**
   - Mark **KEPT** vs **RETURNED** items in the Rider App.
   - Collect digital payment via dynamic Razorpay QR or collect exact Cash on Delivery (COD) for kept items + delivery fee.
4. **Mandatory Return Inspection & In-App Photo Proof:**
   - Verify unkept garments have original brand tags attached and show no damage or stains.
   - **Capture clear photo proof** of returned garments using the in-app camera (`ReturnItemCamera`).
   - Enter customer's 4-digit return OTP.
5. **Immediate Return Trip to Merchant:** Promptly transport unkept merchandise back to the merchant store. Complete return inspection with the merchant and collect the merchant return OTP.

---

## 5. Cash on Delivery (COD) & Fiduciary Remittance
- **Fiduciary Trust:** All cash collected from customers is held in legal trust for FlashFits.
- **Remittance SLA:** Collected cash must be remitted via the in-app UPI payment portal or bank branch deposit upon reaching the ₹5,000 cash-in-hand limit or by end of shift.
- **Zero Tolerance:** Withholding customer cash constitutes criminal breach of trust, leading to immediate account termination and police escalation.

---

## 6. Code of Conduct, Zero Harassment & Road Safety
- **Zero Harassment Policy:** FlashFits enforces absolute zero tolerance for sexual harassment, verbal abuse, stalking, discriminatory comments, or unwanted communication with customers or store staff.
- **Customer Privacy Protection:** Saving customer phone numbers to personal devices, calling customers post-delivery, or sharing customer addresses is strictly forbidden.
- **Road Safety & Attire:** Helmets are mandatory at all times. Riders must obey all traffic lights and speed limits. Operating while impaired by alcohol or drugs results in instant termination.

---

## 7. Parcel Safety, Zero Tampering & Loss Liability
- **Strict Anti-Tampering:** Opening zip covers, trying on garments, replacing items, removing tags, or pilfering merchandise is strictly prohibited.
- **Willful Loss Liability:** Confirmed parcel theft or intentional damage will be recovered from pending payouts or security deposits, and reported to law enforcement.

---

## 8. Earnings, Wait-Time Compensation & Weekly Settlements
- **Earnings Formula:** Base pickup/drop fee + per-kilometer distance allowance + doorstep trial wait compensation + peak-hour surge bonuses.
- **Settlement Schedule:** Automated direct bank transfer on a weekly cycle (or daily express payouts where applicable). Applicable TDS under Section 194C is deducted as mandated by law.

---

## 9. GPS Telemetry, Background Location & Privacy Consent
- **Operational Location Tracking:** The FlashFits Rider App accesses your device location (including in background) only while you are marked **"Online"** or executing an active order delivery.
- **Telemetry Purpose:** To provide real-time tracking for customers, calculate accurate distance pay, and dispatch emergency roadside assistance.
- **Tracking Off Duty:** GPS telemetry streaming stops immediately when you switch to **"Offline"** or log out.

---

## 10. Emergency SOS, Roadside Assistance & Accidental Insurance
- **Emergency SOS:** In-app one-touch emergency trigger directly notifies FlashFits Safety Operations and local emergency services.
- **Accidental Safety Net:** All active on-shift delivery partners are insured under the FlashFits Partner Accidental Care policy covering medical expenses and accidental disability.

---

## 11. Account Deactivation, Breaches & Appeals Process
- **Grounds for Deactivation:** Low fulfillment rate, severe SLA breaches, unremitted COD, customer harassment, or parcel tampering.
- **Grievance & Appeals:** Partners may appeal deactivations within 7 business days by submitting a dispute ticket to `rider-support@flashfits.com`.
